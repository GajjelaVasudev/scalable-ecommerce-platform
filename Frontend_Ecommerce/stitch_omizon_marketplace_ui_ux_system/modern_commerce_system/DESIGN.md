## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a high-tier multi-category marketplace aesthetic, balancing vast catalog density with luxury restraint. It moves deliberately away from discount-driven commerce through deep structural violet accents, tactile clarity, and disciplined visual rhythm. The emotional posture conveys operational reliability, refined curation, and effortless speed.

The design movement synthesizes **Modern High-End Utility** with **Subtle Structural Architecture**:
- Content-forward, clutter-free layouts that elevate consumer electronics, editorial beauty, lifestyle goods, and everyday essentials with equal poise.
- Structural Midnight Violet containers anchoring critical global chrome, including primary navigation bars, mega-menus, sticky bottom checkout sheets, and filter bars.
- A unified typographic foundation led by Plus Jakarta Sans (with Manrope fallback) delivering soft geometric precision, confident heading weights, and highly readable body text.
- High-contrast, tactile micro-affordances designed for rapid basket assembly, instant checkout actions, and clear faceted search navigation.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by a deep violet brand foundation paired with bright Porcelain neutrals for daytime clarity and an Obsidian Violet mode for immersive shopping contexts.

### Key Roles
- **Electric Violet (`#762FF2`):** Primary action color. Governs high-intent buttons, active filter pills, focus halos, and progress indicators.
- **Royal Purple (`#4C2ACB`):** Secondary accent. Drives interactive hover states, secondary links, and selected criteria.
- **Midnight Violet (`#190B46`):** Structural anchor. Powers global navigation headers, high-contrast dark banners, and transactional footers.
- **Porcelain (`#F7F7FB`):** Base canvas background. Provides soft separation against crisp pure white surface containers without glare.

### Surface System
- **Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Product cards, flyout drawers, elevated sheets, and dialogs.
- **Dividers & Structural Borders (`#E8E6F0`):** 1px borders between faceted filters, table rows, and grid partitions.
- **Typography Colors:** Primary content renders in Ink (`#211D31`), secondary metadata in `#655E7A`, and low-emphasis disclaimers in `#928B9F`.

## Typography

The design system standardizes on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles, supported by **Manrope** as the primary system fallback (`font-family: 'Plus Jakarta Sans', 'Manrope', sans-serif`). All serif and display display faces have been replaced with this geometric, contemporary sans-serif.

### Weight Distribution
- **Headings & Display Titles:** Rendered strictly in Semibold (`600`) and Bold (`700`) to provide authoritative editorial structure while preserving warm geometry.
- **Buttons, Actions & Navigation Items:** Rendered in Medium (`500`) or Semibold (`600`) for balanced optical weight inside interactive targets.
- **Body Text & Explanatory Copy:** Rendered in Regular (`400`) to guarantee high legibility across long product overviews and dense specification sheets.

### Numerical & Price Rendering
- All pricing, SKUs, inventory counts, and order totals require `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums lining-nums` to ensure exact column alignment within grids, order summaries, and checkout flows.
- Currency symbols maintain the exact font-weight of their following numerals with standard non-breaking inline spacing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout system employs an 8pt base grid fluidly adapting within a maximum centered desktop canvas of 1440px.

### Responsive Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column grid, `gutter: 1.5rem (24px)`, `margin: 2rem (32px)`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column grid, `gutter: 1rem (16px)`, `margin: 1.5rem (24px)`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `gutter: 0.75rem (12px)`, `margin: 1rem (16px)`.

### Grid & Density Rules
- Standard product grids feature 4 columns on wide screens, 3 or 2 columns on tablet, and dual-column (`repeat(2, 1fr)`) layout on mobile.
- Left-hand faceted filter columns maintain a fixed `280px` footprint on desktop and transform into a sliding bottom drawer on screen widths under 1024px.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses crisp boundary lines paired with delicate ambient shadows tinted by Midnight Violet. Surfaces remain structural and planar.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Porcelain (`#F7F7FB`). Flat with structural boundaries defined by `1px solid #E8E6F0`.
- **Level 1 (Card Resting):** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E8E6F0`, box shadow `0 1px 3px rgba(25, 11, 70, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(25, 11, 70, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover):** Box shadow `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(25, 11, 70, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(25, 11, 70, 0.04)`, border shifts to `1px solid rgba(118, 47, 242, 0.25)` with a `-2px` vertical translation.
- **Level 3 (Flyouts & Dropdowns):** Box shadow `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(25, 11, 70, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(25, 11, 70, 0.06)`, border `1px solid #E8E6F0`.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Checkout Sheets):** Box shadow `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(25, 11, 70, 0.22)`. Backdrops use `rgba(25, 11, 70, 0.5)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(6px)`.

## Shapes

The system implements a balanced rounded shape language (`roundedness: 2`):

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Applied to buttons, text fields, search bars, quantity adjusters, and form controls.
- **Container Radius (`0.75rem` to `1rem` / 12px–16px):** Applied to product cards, floating panels, dialogs, and media carousels.
- **Pill Radius (`9999px`):** Reserved for category tags, promotional chips, and count badges.
- **Concentric Rule:** Internal media elements nested within a `12px` card corner must maintain an inner radius of `8px` or `6px` to preserve visual harmony.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `Electric Violet (#762FF2)`, text `#FFFFFF`, font-weight `500` (Medium), border-radius `8px`. Hover shifts to `Royal Purple (#4C2ACB)`. Active scale: `0.985`.
- **Secondary:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E8E6F0`, text `Ink (#211D31)`, font-weight `500`. Hover brings surface `#F7F7FB` and border color `#762FF2`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Borderless, text `Royal Purple (#4C2ACB)`. Hover invokes `rgba(118, 47, 242, 0.08)` surface fill.
- **Navigation Buttons:** Rendered in `label-md` or `title-sm` with Medium (`500`) weight.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Neutral:** `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E8E6F0`, `Ink` text at `label-md`.
- **Active Filter:** Midnight Violet (`#190B46`) or Electric Violet (`#762FF2`) background with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **Promo Badges:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), height `22px`, font `label-sm` in Semibold (`600`).

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, `12px` radius, `1px solid #E8E6F0` perimeter border.
- **Image Module:** Aspect ratio `1:1` or `3:4` on a neutral `#F7F7FB` background with embedded wishlist button.
- **Typography Placement:** Category/Brand at `body-sm` (muted), Product Title clamped to 2 lines at `title-sm` (Semibold), and Price block formatted with `price-card` (Bold) using tabular figures.

### Form Inputs & Quantity Selectors
- **Inputs:** Height `44px`, radius `8px`, border `1px solid #E8E6F0`, font `body-md` (Regular). Active focus yields `1px solid #762FF2` with a `3px rgba(118, 47, 242, 0.15)` ring.
- **Quantity Steppers:** Segmented `36px` height container with `-` and `+` triggers enclosing a tabular numeral quantity display at `title-sm`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size `18x18px`, `border: 1.5px solid #C3BCD7`. Checked state fills with `#762FF2` displaying a crisp white indicator.
